MANIFESTO

Every frame has a story.

In Frame is an independent digital archive dedicated to photographic stillness, architectural harmony, and the quiet visual textures of our contemporary world.

Photography is rarely about sheer spectacle; it is about paying attention. This archive documents the interaction between natural light, structural geometry, and fleeting human moments across continents. Each frame is selected not for transient trends, but for its enduring rhythm and ability to provoke contemplation.

Designed with generous whitespace and respectful typography, In Frame presents photography as a curated exhibition rather than an endless ephemeral feed. Crafted as part of the CodeAlpha Frontend Development Internship (Task 1).
